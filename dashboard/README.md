# Strait of Hormuz: Oil Flow, Tanker Traffic & Price Trends

## Project Overview

This Power BI project analyzes oil flow, tanker traffic, oil prices, and daily oil flow–price elasticity in the Strait of Hormuz.

The dashboard explores how changes in oil transportation activity are associated with changes in oil prices during the selected analysis period.

## Analytical Approach

Daily percentage changes in oil flow and oil prices were used to examine the relationship between transportation activity and market price movements.

Oil flow–price elasticity was calculated to measure how oil prices changed relative to changes in oil flow during the analyzed period.

Positive elasticity indicates that oil flow and oil prices moved in the same direction, while negative elasticity indicates movement in opposite directions.

## Important Analytical Note

The observed oil flow–price relationship represents an association within the analyzed dataset and should not automatically be interpreted as causation.

Oil prices may also be affected by factors such as:

- Global oil supply and demand
- Geopolitical developments
- OPEC+ production decisions
- Market expectations
- Inventory levels
- Currency movements
- Broader macroeconomic conditions
